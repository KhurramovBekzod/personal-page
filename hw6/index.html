About MercyMed

A clinic that grew out of mobile clinics

MercyMed is a private medical centre in Bishkek. We provide high-quality medical care based on modern medical knowledge and international experience.

For over 10 years our team has run medical consultations in different regions of Kyrgyzstan. The clinic in Bishkek opened in October 2023 to continue and expand this work, and to give patients a place for follow-up care.

What “evidence-based” means for you

Our decisions are based on current research and international experience, not only on habit. We try to avoid medicines and tests that are not really needed, so you do not pay for treatment that will not help.

Mobile clinics

Invited by villages and local health centres, our team travels with doctors, a nurse and medicines to hold clinics for one day or several days.

Medical education

Medical students have trained with us for several weeks, and we hope to train resident doctors soon.

For organisations

Schools, companies and embassies can work with us on yearly staff check-ups. See the offer under Medical Services.

Medical Services

What we offer

We are an outpatient clinic: we see patients during the day and do not keep anyone overnight. Diagnostics and lab tests are arranged after a consultation with one of our doctors.

Consultations

  • Family medicine for adults and children
  • Pediatrics
  • Gynecology
  • Pulmonology
  • Physical therapy
  • Preventive check-ups for children, women and men
  • Pregnancy and birth preparation with our certified doula

Diagnostics and lab tests

  • Ultrasound
  • EKG (heart test)
  • Vision testing
  • Hearing test (audiometry)
  • Laboratory tests on site, including anemia and diabetes screening

Documents and certificates

  • For school
  • For flights and travel
  • For employment
  • For insurance

For schools, companies and embassies

We offer yearly staff check-ups and special deals for students. Contact us to discuss a partnership.
